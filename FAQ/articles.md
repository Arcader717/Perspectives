# How Articles are written

I, Online? (Arcader717), and my friend Very_Cool_Bird, will each provide their own perspective. The Left and Right sections will follow these rules:

- Use sources that mainly correspond to each side
- Use sources known for high factuality
- Provide perspectives of the people who support this side, for this topic
- Attempt to provide demographic charts for those who support this side, for this topic

For Neutral sections, each must be written with these rules in mind:

- Use multiple sources
- Verify every claim with accurate reports
- Identify each perspective
- Identify the reason for a split
- Minimize opinions and, if necessary, higlight any opinions
- Allow the reader to choose their own side, without pushing them to pick a certain side.
- Morals mean very little on the scale of politics, do not use morals as a reason. Consider the average person, who has no opinion on this topic.
- Show well reported and under reported sides for both the Left and Right sections.
- Use simple words, with little political background.
- Provide previous rulings if necessary, and interpret the letter of the law exactly as it is written, following the exact legal definitions. 

Neutral sections are supposed to be very in between, showing the perspective as if an outside spectator.
