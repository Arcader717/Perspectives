# Perspectives

A simple project that gives three views on political topics. 

## Left

I lean left, so I will try to write these articles from my perspective and opinions.

## Right

A friend of mine leans right, so he will give his opinion and perspective in these articles.

## Center

Though difficult to combine perspectives, my friend and I will work on this part together, and hopefully explain why there is a split in opinion. We want to let the reader understand why other people might think differently for something you believe strongly in. 

## Moderation through community

By making this project entirely Open Source, it will show that nothing is censored. After adding a page, nothing will be removed, and any previous entries or edits will be available through the git commit history. Any inaccurate information, sources, or typos can be pointed out, in issues, or the comments below each article.

## Control of each perspective

Since people are naturally biased, both me and my friend will tell our perspectives, and will not change the wording of the other section. No section will come first. Neutral perspective is only for the absolute facts. We must come to an agreement on the wording and exact facts given in the article.
